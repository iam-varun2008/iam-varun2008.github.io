FAQ

Got any
questions?

Why Webflow instead of custom code?

Webflow gives you a platform your team can manage after launch, with the flexibility to build things that feel fully custom. I extend it with custom code and integrations when needed.

Do you handle design, or only development?

Development is my core strength, but I have a strong eye for design and regularly collaborate with designers to refine layouts, spacing, and visual quality.

Already have a Webflow site that needs work?

Absolutely. I can audit what you have, identify what is holding it back, and build a clear plan to improve performance, structure, CMS, or new sections.

What does ongoing support look like?

You get a dedicated block of development hours each month for features, campaign updates, performance fixes, and CMS changes.

What’s the process from start to launch?

We align on goals and scope, then I build in stages and share progress as I go so feedback stays easy and nothing drifts.

How do you handle revisions and feedback?

Revisions are built into the process. I share progress at key stages so adjustments happen while they are still easy.

Do you work under NDA?

Yes. I’m happy to sign an NDA before discussing project details and treat every client’s work with discretion.

Not sure which plan fits your project?

No stress. Reach out at nenad@popadic.co and tell me what you have in mind. I’ll help you choose the right option.
